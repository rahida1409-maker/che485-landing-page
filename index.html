      Comparison of Closed-Ended and Open-Ended Labs

      Closed-Ended Lab (CE):Focus on exact directions, a set procedure and tight control.
      1Read the directed lab manual
      2Follow it and run the experiment
      3Get your raw data signed
      Open-Ended Lab (OE):Encourages creativity, freedom to experiment and exploration.
      1Plan your own procedure from the problem given
      2Consult your lecturer, one week before
      3Run the experiment

          Please remember

          Important rules

          1. 01

            Attendance compulsory

            No attendance, no report submission. No replacement lab without a valid reason.

          2. 02

            Lab coat and covered shoes

            No sandals or shorts. No mobile phone in the lab.

          3. 03

            Raw data signed before you leave

            Your lecturer verifies and signs the data sheet.

          4. 04

            Consult one week early

            For open-ended (OE) experiments.

          5. 05

            Submit within two weeks

            Directly to your lecturer, before 5 pm. Not to the FKK office or the technician office.

          6. 06

            Late submission or plagiarism

            A penalty applies.
